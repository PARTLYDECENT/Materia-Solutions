🍳 SDF KITCHEN 3D

A full procedural raymarched culinary simulator — fully generated with signed distance equations and dynamic physical interactions.

  • Grab & Drag in 3D: Click & drag ingredients (Eggs, Patties, Shrimp, Onions, Cheese, Buns) straight from the counter into the Pan or Cutting Board.
  • Direct 3D Action: Click the knife to slice onions, tap sizzling pan items to flip them in mid-air, and drag cooked food directly onto the serving plate!
  • Seasoning & Heat: Shake salt 🧂 on cooking food for bonus flavor points and adjust the stove heat.
  • Free Camera: Drag anywhere on empty counter/space with right click or left click to orbit the camera; scroll/pinch to zoom.
