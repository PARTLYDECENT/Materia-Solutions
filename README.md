# 🍪 MATERIA COOKIES

> **"Welcome to the future of baking."**

MATERIA COOKIES is a venture-backed, high-tech food brand startup that is disrupting the confectionery market. By merging advanced computational flavor profiling, automated batch baking, and real-time synthesis, we are creating the next big thing in food tech. 

This project serves as an interactive showcase of our tech-noir generative baking pipeline, real-time auditory synthesis, and immersive user experiences.

---

## 💎 Core Experience

### 1. Cinematic Intro (`index.html`)
A 3,000-particle CPU-projected 3D sequence that morphs through 7 timed phases.
- **Brand Transition**: The stardust particles dynamically morph to spell **MATERIA COOKIES** in heavy and sleek typography.
- **Text-to-Speech**: A cinematic, deep-pitched voice announces: *"Welcome to the future of baking. Materia Cookies."*
- **Pure Synthesis Score**: A grandiose orchestral score synthesized in real-time using raw Web Audio oscillators—zero audio files loaded.
- **Dynamic Camera**: Zoom, pan, and screen-shake effects synchronized with audio impacts.

### 2. Confectionery Flavor Store (`STORE.HTML`)
A premium glassmorphism storefront displaying six of our flagship computational cookie batches:
- 🔥 **SCARLET SPICE** (Fire - Baked on Mass)
- 💾 **CYBER MINT** (Cyber - Vacuum Sealed)
- 🌑 **VOID COCOA** (Void - In Production)
- 🌬️ **AERO LEMON** (Aero - Batch Ready)
- 🌈 **PRISM SUGAR** (Prism - Styling Soon)
- ❄️ **GHOST FROST** (Frost - Vacuum Sealed)
- 🌀 **GRAVITY FUDGE** (Gravity - Vacuum Sealed)
- 🧠 **NEURAL BERRY** (Neural - Fresh Batch)

### 3. The Baking Entity AI (`entity.js`)
An autonomous, raymarched SDF helper entity that inhabits the background and assists in flavor balancing.
- **Evolutionary Forms**: Morphs between 6 distinct forms: *Orb, Spider, Mass, Skull, Crown, and Parasite*.
- **Autonomous AI**: Decides when to watch, hover, mutate, or recede into the cooling chambers based on internal state logic.
- **Raymarched Volumes**: Rendered without traditional mesh geometry for a perfectly smooth, organic feel.

### 4. Confectionery Glitch System (`animator.js`)
The storefront periodically "scrambles" from its advanced product line-up into classic cookie varieties.
- **FLIP Transitions**: Cards reshuffle their positions with smooth physics.
- **Text Scramble**: Real-time decode animation as "VOID COCOA" becomes "MACARON ROYALE".
- **Micro-Glitches**: Random brief distortions, skews, and text corruption maintain a "haunted" feel.

### 5. Generative Soundtrack Suite
15 distinct procedural audio engines that compose music live inside the browser:
- **Materia I (Prime)**: Original ambient engine in C minor.
- **Materia II (Dark)**: Industrial generative soundscape.
- **Materia III (Luminous)**: Ethereal Ionian/Lydian harmony shifts.
- **Materia IV**: Cinematic Orchestral engine.
- **Materia V**: Hyper-aggressive industrial techno.
- **Materia VI**: Dark industrial Phrygian ambient.
- **Materia VII**: Ethereal cinematic synthwave.
- **Materia VIII**: Glitch Hop fractal wavefolder.
- **Materia IX**: Acoustic Singularity comb resonator.
- **Materia X**: Cybernetic Hivemind ring-mod techno.
- **Materia XI (Solutus)**: 5-movement generative symphony.
- **Materia XII (Xenomorph)**: Chaotic bio-mechanical FM feedback.
- **Materia XIII (Europa)**: High-fidelity synthwave with sidechain compression.
- **Materia XIV (Cryo)**: Cryogenic sub-aquatic ambient engine.
- **Materia XV (Golden)**: [NEW] Warm, bouncy pastry-pop lofi beat celebrating our startup launch.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

| Layer | Technology |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Graphics** | Three.js, GLSL (Raymarching, SDF, Gyroid Noise, FBM) |
| **Audio** | Web Audio API (FM Synthesis, LFOs, Feedback Delay Networks) |
| **Animation** | CPU-based Particle Physics, CSS Keyframes, RequestAnimationFrame |
| **Styling** | Tailwind CSS, Glassmorphism, Vanilla CSS |
| **Logic** | ES6 JavaScript, Autonomous State Machines |
